I co-lead a work package in BEAGLE, an EU Horizon project extending biodiversity data collection across **all modalities** — environmental DNA (eDNA), acoustics, and camera traps. By combining these complementary data sources with AI, we aim to build a more complete picture of biodiversity status and trends to support conservation and policy decisions at a European scale.
